Where the Chicory Root Inulin in SodaTide Comes From

Chicory is a plant grown across Europe and North America, and inulin is extracted from its root.

Why Chicory Root Inulin Is in the SodaTide Formula

It is the first fibre named, positioned to feed gut bacteria, with no treatment claim attached. SodaTide is a dietary supplement, not a treatment for memory loss or any condition, and no result is promised.

Ask Your Doctor About Chicory Root Inulin in SodaTide If

You have a sensitive stomach or a digestive condition, are allergic to chicory or related plants such as ragweed, take medicine, or are pregnant or nursing. Inulin can cause gas, bloating or loose stools, especially at first. Digestive diseases: NIDDK.
